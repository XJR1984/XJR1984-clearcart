## Video description — ready to complete

Most shopping begins with a product search. ClearCart begins with your goal, your budget, and what you already own.

In this working prototype, a small trained AI classifier recognizes supported starter tasks, while an exact-cent optimizer creates an explainable cart from a synthetic demo catalog. You can adjust the budget, exclude items you own, review the reasons, and choose whether to start a **PayPal sandbox** checkout.

This is a demonstration of a user-controlled payment flow. The products and prices are illustrative, transactions use test funds, and no goods are sold or shipped. **[Before publishing, ensure any claim about completed live PayPal capture matches real recorded provider evidence.]**
