## Exact rule excerpts supporting the plan

**Deadline, section 1:** “Submission Period: Thursday Oct 1, 2026 (9:15 am Pacific Time) – Thursday Nov 12, 2026 (12:00 pm Pacific Time).”

**Required integration, section 4:** “Entrants must build or update an application that integrates the PayPal developer platform (using the free sandbox environment) along with an AI tool, model, or platform of their choice.”

**AI choice, section 4:** “Any AI tool works, as long as PayPal integration is central to the project.”

**Working demo, section 4:** “Your project does not need to be publicly hosted or deployed — but judges must be able to actually run or interact with a working build.” Complete setup and run instructions or a hosted demo URL may satisfy this. The same section excludes a design mockup, static prototype, or non-functional demo.

**Repository, section 4:** “The repository must be public and open source by including an open source license file.”

**Video, section 4:** The video “should be less than three (3) minutes” and “must be uploaded to and made publicly visible on YouTube.”

**Judging, section 6:** Technological implementation, design, potential impact, innovation/idea, and presentation are equally weighted. Eligibility and awards remain at the organizers' discretion.

## Interpretation and limits

- The rules support using AI tools and technical assistance, subject to ownership and originality. We disclose Codex's development role rather than implying unaided authorship.
- A small trained classifier is an AI component, but the judges decide whether its integration is meaningful and competitive. No organizer approval of ClearCart has been obtained.
- Synthetic catalog prices demonstrate behavior; they are not verified retail offers. No user study, market-size claim, novelty search, or demonstrated savings claim is included.
- Tests using a simulated PayPal response establish application behavior, not a successfully executed PayPal sandbox transaction. Record those separately.
- Prize totals are opportunity descriptions, not expected income. Some sponsor rewards are service credits or subscriptions.
